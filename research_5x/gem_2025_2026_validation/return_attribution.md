All numbers below are **uncertified retrospective accounting results**, not verified investable performance. Exact GEM is reproducible; the complete point-in-time rank and account reconstruction is not certified.

## Selection, sizing and capital exposure

Actual original first purchases: PLTR February 3, 2025; AMD/STX June 2; MU/WDC July 1; LRCX November 3. In the fresh 2026 account: LRCX/MU/STX/WDC February 2, SNDK March 2, AMD June 1, PLTR October 1. These stocks emerged through the revenue/earnings/margin gates and r63 ordering, not a manually curated list. The continuous account carried positions through January 2026 instead of waiting for its first new basket. This explains why multiplying the two fresh annual-account returns does not equal C's actual compounded return.

The strategy is not an industry leadership classifier. It identified individual storage/memory/semiconductor names; missing historical industry data prevents proof that it identified or allocated to the strongest historical industry. Rank dates and financial inputs show what it knew, not that entries preceded all historical upside. Initial and subsequent entry ranks, costs and holdings are in the audit files; realized losses and rejected/absent-price candidates remain visible.

Five equal slots, whole shares and no leverage generated returns using actual dollars. Original maximum marked stock weight was 26.69% in A/C and 24.14% in B; these exceed 20% targets through between-rebalance drift. Average equity exposure was 85.07%, 79.77% and 89.74%. Initial cash and incomplete baskets reduced exposure. The original has no market-wide bearish regime switch; losses are constrained only through its actual eligibility, monthly rotation and cash rules. A roughly 29% continuous drawdown is meaningful capital risk, not proof of downturn protection.

## Winner concentration and retention

WDC contributed $3,189.64 in A and $7,725.75 in C; SNDK contributed $2,082.46 in B. Top-three stocks supplied 64.27%, 49.19%, and 44.59% of positive stock profits in A/B/C. This denominator counts positive contributions, not total net profit. Removing the largest winner after seeing outcomes gives A +76.44%, B +57.69%, C +286.07%. This is hindsight sensitivity analysis, not a prospective exclusion rule. It demonstrates dependence on exceptional names; it does not establish a causal counterfactual without those industries.

Rank retention lowers A's replay return (+91.07%) but increases B (+87.17%) and C (+379.62%). Weight preservation gives A +101.69%, B +74.19%, C +341.39%; B drawdown worsens to −33.09%. The combination gives A +95.97%, B +79.89%, C +398.68%, with C drawdown −33.91%. Retention can improve compounding but does not consistently improve return or drawdown. All variants are previously documented; none was chosen using these outcomes. Trade counts, costs and exposures in the comparison show whether reduced rotation accompanies each outcome. Since these policies affect realized baskets/capital paths, their differences are not a clean attribution of all original profits solely to retention.

## Costs, delays and failures

At 100bps per side, original replay returns fall to +85.18%, +61.80%, +276.67%. Entry delays have non-monotonic effects; all 1/5/20-session outcomes are preserved. Some longer delays improve results, but no best delay is chosen. Original 2025 minus WDC falls below the 80% target, despite the full replay passing it. Industry exclusion remains blocked.

The maximum modeled trade was 0.00551% of prior-20 quoted daily dollar volume across baseline accounts. This supports small-account capacity only under vendor volume conventions; it cannot certify auction fills, overnight gap execution or removed-security liquidity. No claim of large-capital scalability is made.

## Answers and final classification

1. Exact archived GEM configuration/accounting: reproduced, six metrics identical.
2. Strict PIT performance survival: not established; qualifying financial observations verify, but omitted competitors, historical eligibility/action lineage and execution still prevent certification.
3–5. Narrowed outcomes: as above, explicitly uncertified, with full NAV, cash, trades and position accounting.
6. Retention: mixed, variant-specific improvement with drawdown/concentration tradeoffs.
7. Exceptional stock dependence: substantial; exclusion and top-three attribution quantify it.
8. Sustainable approximately 80% annual growth: promising historical numerical evidence, but only one completed calendar year here; unfinished 2026 is not a second annual success or independent trial. Reused periods, concentration, cost sensitivity and incomplete PIT reconstruction contradict a sustainable-return claim. The +400% stretch target is not established by a roughly 21-month cumulative gain.
9. Independent validation still requires complete PIT competitors and source lineage, correct distribution timing/execution, then a frozen prospective test on genuinely unseen observations. Two years, even fully reconstructed, cannot establish a reliable long-term success probability.

**Final classification: Insufficient data.** The strongest defensible alternative is the exact, accounting-reconciled retrospective replay with verified qualifying SEC inputs and explicitly unresolved universe/execution gaps. It is promising evidence; it is not a certified 80% process.
