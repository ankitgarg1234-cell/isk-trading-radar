# Failure attribution and missed opportunities

## Evidence-backed findings, with limits

The original 75/25 control has a recovery failure before a performance failure can be assessed. Its reconstruction misses the claimed 2024 result by 47.12 percentage points. Changing parameters until totals match would conceal a broken reference rather than validate the control.

The adaptive replay did identify/buy AI-era leaders in 2023: its diagnostic lists NVDA bought six times and MSFT seven times in the full-BEAR control. Yet the continuous portfolio's 2023 annual slice was −4.97% with 46.90% average equity exposure. Ninety-two ATR-stop sales realized −$869.96; 11 monthly resize sales realized +$199.54; ten BEAR-liquidation sales realized +$25.93. Finding winners did not assure early persistent exposure, adequate capital or uninterrupted compounding. These sell-PnL sums describe realized transactions within a real archived account, not a sum of stock percentage returns and not a causal estimate of the return obtainable without those exits.

No-new-buys in BEAR improves that archived slice only to −4.26%; weekly recovery gives −5.92%; narrow leadership tilt gives −5.31%. More adaptive complexity did not consistently repair the gap. Whole-share allocation, timing, repeated stops and idle cash interact; the missing original and uncertified universe preclude assigning the failure to any one cause conclusively.

The growth/margin baseline's archived continuous slices are +29.56% in 2024 and +95.41% in 2025. Only one of those two slices reaches 80%; neither is a new independent January-reset experiment. Excluding the storage/memory cluster (STX/WDC/MU/SNDK) lowers its archived 2025 return to +33.90%. Excluding the current technology sector produces −7.28% in 2025. These are explicitly **hindsight sensitivity experiments**, with imperfect current classification; they are not prospective exclusion rules or a certified strongest-industry test.

The June 2025–June 2026 winner account ends at $51,081.81, +410.82%, with −18.00% drawdown. MU, WDC and STX supply 87.49% of positive attributed profit. It is one selected, later interval, extends outside 2022–2025, and cannot establish 5× repeatability. The published account loses money on ANET (−$63.24) and SNDK (−$91.26); both failures remain in evidence. Its first entries were July 1 after the June 30 signal, not January 2025.

Manual release evidence shows no positive revenue-growth acceleration in all three main June 2025 winners, even though profits/margins improved. An earlier MU release-stage setup in April 2024 lost 28.54% over 252 sessions with −57.82% path drawdown. The monthly first-observed MU screen in March 2024 is a different timing/entry diagnostic; do not conflate its price-only outcome with the April release entry or an account return. Demand recovery, earnings recovery and revenue acceleration are distinct hypotheses.

The archived matched holding ablations preserve initial baskets. Untouched holding at 20 bps has a +33.00% median across 22 overlapping annual starts, a −5.30% worst start, and −34.72% largest account drawdown. Monthly caps reduce extreme drifting concentration but can trim compounding winners; even monthly cap25 can drift above 25% between checks. Static baskets without replacements do not test a continuously adapting strategy.

## What cannot be attributed yet

Whether entries preceded most upside, whether rotation consistently traded stronger holdings for weaker candidates, and how much missing capital allocation cost in 2022–2025 cannot be estimated without certified contemporaneous shortlists and complete trade/holding books. Ex post winner labels must not change eligibility. The AI-2023, technology-2024 and storage/memory-2025 leadership hypotheses are recorded as falsifiable questions, not forced sector labels. Missing opportunities and unsuccessful contemporaneous candidates must remain in the full candidate audit once data is acquired.

The requested adverse-gap, reduced-liquidity and industry-cap stresses were not run. No favorable conclusion is inferred from their absence. Costs alone do not invalidate the winner: its archived 100-bps result remains +401.83% before terminal liquidation. Conversely, that result does not validate delisting treatment, publication-time selection or repeatability.

## Strongest defensible alternative to 80%

Growth/margin selection and restrained winner retention are **promising research candidates** for improved compounding, supported by archived matched holding evidence and the 2025 cluster signal. There is no defensible sustainable annual return estimate. The +33% overlapping holding median is descriptive, not a forecast, four-year CAGR or independent annual median. The remaining gap is broad bearish/recovery-period validation, correct original control, complete PIT data, concentration dependence and genuinely unseen evidence.
