Research integrity and next admissible experiment

Data lookahead concerns information unavailable at the historical decision. Research overfitting concerns designing rules after seeing outcomes and calling those same outcomes independent evidence. Repairing the former does not repair the latter.

Proposed chronological plan: 2010–2018 training, 2019–2021 validation, 2022–2025 retrospective evaluation, followed by a genuinely unobserved period beginning after the full rule/data freeze. Training data and adaptive industry rules are not yet available; no fitted or frozen D/E is claimed. All previously viewed 2022–2025 years remain retrospective even after data repairs. Four calendar years cannot establish a reliable long-term success probability.

Each future signal requires immutable source lineage and availability timestamps strictly before the signal. Prices must come from the preceding completed session; orders execute later using raw marketable prices and explicit costs. Dividend receivables/cash, spinoff distributions and delisting consideration require actual economic capital accounting. Historical consensus is omitted until vintage data exists. One changed variable per experiment; no year-specific exceptions or threshold changes after test results without exploratory classification.

Evidence-based decision

Insufficient data. Growth/earnings/margin selection and winner retention are promising research candidates, but no sustainable annual return estimate is defensible. The +33% overlapping holding median is the strongest descriptive alternative in this evidence package, not a long-term return target. The gap to +80% is unresolved bearish/recovery-regime performance, exceptional-winner dependence, correct original control, complete PIT inputs and independently unseen testing.
