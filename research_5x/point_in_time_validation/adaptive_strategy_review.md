# Adaptive strategy review

**Central 2022–2025 claim: Insufficient data.** Approximately 80% repeatable annual growth and repeatable 5× growth are not established. A fixed process's ability to adapt, retain winners and protect capital has not been independently validated across the requested four years.

## What the available evidence says

The adaptive market-state replay measures trailing price breadth and mega-cap health, with deterministic market states. It can change exposure and recovery timing without hardcoding years. Its recorded 2023 AI-leader purchases demonstrate that leader identification alone does not imply successful portfolio capture. It is neither the reconciled original A nor the requested sector/industry D; improvements within that reconstruction cannot be transferred to the original portfolio.

The archived growth/earnings/margin screen identifies storage/memory during 2025 without an explicit ticker whitelist, but selection inputs retain the archive's vintage/scope/universe problems. Matched holding experiments suggest that avoiding repeated resizing can retain upside. Drift produces extreme company/cluster weights and larger drawdowns. These are research observations, not proof that the same process reliably discovers the next industry.

Expectations acceleration/deterioration remains untestable without historical estimate snapshots. Price/fundamental disagreement can be investigated using trailing prices and published results, but cannot be relabeled as an analyst-expectation test. No missing factor is replaced by modern financials, contemporary sector assignments, or remembered historical winners.

## Chronological development protocol

Proposed training: 2010–2018. Proposed validation: 2019–2021. Retrospective evaluation: 2022–2025. These date partitions are an acquisition/research plan, **not available trained/frozen strategies or completed folds**. The selected twelve-stock earlier-price probe is unsuitable training data. Acquire full historical universes and release vintages before specifying the adaptive industry/rank/retention thresholds. Specify candidate parameter families, objective and drawdown tradeoffs using only training observations. Select on chronological validation, commit immutable rules and source hashes, then evaluate all requested accounts without revision.

All requested years have been seen by prior research, so repaired 2022–2025 tests remain retrospective. An untouched period must start after the eventual full process/data freeze; this audit does not schedule trades or start paper deployment. Pre-registration today cannot manufacture historical independence. No binomial probability estimate should treat the four years or 22 overlapping starts as independent identically distributed trials.

No test-year tuning or year-specific exception is permitted.

## Required robustness queue

Higher costs/slippage (100 bps and decomposed fee/slippage components); entry delays +1/+5/+20 sessions **in addition to baseline next-session execution**; predeclared price-derived regimes; largest winner and strongest industry exclusions explicitly hindsight-only; separate position/sector/industry caps; adverse overnight gap execution at marketable open rather than unavailable stop levels; reduced liquidity with prior-known ADV participation caps, partial fills and unfilled cash. Do not assume liquidity caps are training parameters until historical volume/universe and cost data are reliable.

The archive includes delayed execution, higher-cost, retention and correlated-exclusion diagnostics, indexed as 48 inherited continuous scenarios. It does not establish the entire requested stress suite or its effectiveness across 2022–2025. Delays improving some archived returns are outcomes, not reasons to tune an optimal delay. Frozen signal selection must remain unchanged during delay stress; membership/marketability can suppress a delayed fill without hindsight replacement.

## Ten sustainability questions

1. Independent requested annual accounts reaching +80%: **unavailable**, not zero. The archived B continuous slices reach it in one of two complete available years (2025 only).
2. Median requested independent annual return: unavailable. Archived static-basket medians are not substitutes.
3. Worst requested annual result: unavailable. Proxy 2023 adaptive losses and MU false positives contradict a simple all-regime success narrative.
4. Continuous January 2022–December 2025 CAGR: unavailable for A–F. No annual slices from different accounts are spliced together.
5. Full/annual requested drawdowns: unavailable. Archived retention and growth portfolios exhibit substantial drawdown/concentration tradeoffs.
6. Exceptional-company dependence: strongly suggested by the winner's 87.49% top-three positive PnL and cluster-exclusion deterioration.
7. Sector leadership adaptation: partial diagnostic observations; no historical industry validation.
8. Realistic cost/delay resilience: some favorable and unfavorable inherited diagnostics, not a complete certified stress pass.
9. SPY outperformance across regimes: unestablished under identical certified assumptions. Missing benchmark runs remain blank.
10. Sustainable +80%: no independently validated support. Four historical calendar years, even with clean data, cannot establish a reliable long-term success probability.

**Final classification: Insufficient data.** Promising growth/margin/retention hypotheses warrant further validation; no sustainable numerical return alternative is justified by this record.
