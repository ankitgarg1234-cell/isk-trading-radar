5. Broader history and statistical honesty

The public-price probe retrieved 42,264 daily rows across 12 surviving case series from 2010–2023. WBA andPXDfailed. Allsnapshotoptions and hashes are preserved. This extends price availability, not the historical screening universe. Reliable dated membership, delisting/merger proceeds, and released-vintage quarterly fundamentals remain required. Historical consensus remains unavailable.

The research has only 21 maturedmonthly factor cohorts and 22 complete annual monthly starts fromtwo start years. Repeated issuers and overlapping years are dependent. Cohort 12-month block intervals are descriptive with inadequate cycle coverage; binomial independent-trial probabilities are not reported. All periods 2024–2026 were examined previously. No statistical technique turns reused history into an untouched holdout.

A valid subsequent evaluation must freeze rules on earlier released information, purge any training label ending after the next decision boundary, embargo 20 actual SPY sessions where training labels overlap, and evaluate previously unseen dates. Broad daily-funded arbitrary date accounts and remaining capital/gap/liquidity fragility tests are pending.
