# MU and STX: earnings acceleration before the June 2025 selection

The source record supports a recovery in profitability and cloud-related product demand. It does **not** support a simple rule requiring positive revenue-growth acceleration at the winning account's June 30, 2025 selection. The latest observable MU revenue growth slowed by 1.71 percentage points versus the preceding quarter; STX slowed by 19.00 points. Both nevertheless had higher margins than a year earlier and management guides for higher next-quarter revenue. These are descriptive observations from selected winners, not evidence that the later stock gains were predictable.

The packet contains all ten successive quarterly releases for each issuer dated in 2023 through June 2025: **20 release events**, 20 rows with GAAP and adjusted profitability, CFO, guidance and available SBC bridges, and two latest preselection 10-Qs independently matched to SEC acceptance. Quarter-end and release dates are separate. The conservative availability date is the day after each release; the owning analysis must align this to a trading session. Exact intraday publication times are not certified for all releases. Only financial facts and short paraphrases are retained; whole releases are not copied.

## What was known at selection

MU's June 25, 2025 release reported $9.301bn revenue, +36.56% YoY and +15.50% sequentially. GAAP operating margin was 23.32%, up 12.76 points YoY; adjusted margin was 26.77%. Adjusted diluted EPS was $1.91 versus a previously issued management midpoint of $1.57. This is a **beat against company guidance**, not an analyst-consensus earnings surprise. The new next-quarter revenue guide was $10.7bn ± $0.3bn, with adjusted EPS $2.50 ± $0.15 and adjusted gross margin 42% ± 1 point. [Original release](https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2025/Micron-Technology-Inc--Reports-Results-for-the-Third-Quarter-of-Fiscal-2025-06-25-2025/default.aspx)

MU's same-quarter 10-Q was accepted June 25 at 18:50:42 Eastern and has a June 26 filing date. Its original financial statement corroborates the release. Its MD&A separates DRAM from NAND: DRAM's YoY selling prices increased in the mid-20% range, whereas NAND's declined in the low-20% range. Sequential NAND pricing also declined in the high-single-digit range despite rising shipments. HBM mix and manufacturing cost reductions supported margins. A blanket claim that all memory prices were accelerating would contradict this evidence. [SEC acceptance index](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312525000021/0000723125-25-000021-index.html), [original 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312525000021/mu-20250529.htm)

STX's April 29, 2025 release reported $2.160bn revenue, +30.51% YoY but −7.10% sequentially. GAAP operating margin was 19.95%, up 11.31 points YoY; adjusted margin was 23.47%. Adjusted EPS $1.90 reached the top of its previous $1.70 ± $0.20 company guide. The next-quarter guide was $2.40bn ± $0.15bn and adjusted EPS $2.40 ± $0.20. Management cited healthy capacity-storage demand and its HAMR ramp. Gross/operating margin guidance was not provided numerically in the release. [Original release](https://investors.seagate.com/news/news-details/2025/Seagate-Technology-Reports-Fiscal-Third-Quarter-2025-Financial-Results/default.aspx)

STX's 10-Q was accepted May 2 at 16:09:12 Eastern. Mass-capacity shipments were 133 exabytes versus 140 the previous quarter and 88 a year earlier. Rounded HDD price per terabyte was $14/$14/$15 respectively; this mix-sensitive measure does not identify contract pricing by product. Management attributed weaker sequential revenue to temporary production constraints and seasonal end markets, while describing improved nearline cloud demand YoY. The MD&A percentage table prints 21% current operating margin, inconsistent with its $431m/$2,160m dollar ratio; the packet retains the discrepancy and uses the financial-statement ratio, consistent with release 20.0%. [SEC acceptance index](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1137789/000113778925000075/0001137789-25-000075-index.html), [original 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1137789/000113778925000075/stx-20250328.htm)

## Earlier signals and the MU counterexample

MU management described improving inventory and supply-demand conditions in March 2023, then a believed revenue trough in June 2023. These statements accompanied deep losses and an explicitly disclosed Chinese regulatory headwind. Recovery was a management expectation, not a realized profitable inflection at those dates. [March release](https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2023/Micron-Technology-Inc--Reports-Results-for-the-Second-Quarter-of-Fiscal-2023-03-28-2023/default.aspx), [June release](https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2023/Micron-Technology-Inc--Reports-Results-for-the-Third-Quarter-of-Fiscal-2023-06-28-2023/default.aspx)

The reconstructed MU release sequence first shows positive YoY revenue growth in December 2023; March 2024 then shows positive GAAP and adjusted operating income. However, fiscal Q2 2024 GAAP profit included a $200m patent cross-license gain, a tax catch-up benefit, and $382m lower cost from previously written-down inventory. These items were knowable from the release itself. Adjusted gross margin retained that inventory benefit; removing SBC does not isolate demand-related operating leverage. [December release](https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2023/Micron-Technology-Inc--Reports-Results-for-the-First-Quarter-of-Fiscal-2024-12-20-2023/default.aspx), [March 2024 release and notes](https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2024/Micron-Technology-Inc--Reports-Results-for-the-Second-Quarter-of-Fiscal-2024-03-20-2024/default.aspx)

The owning quantitative study finds that MU qualified on April 30, 2024 with +57.7% revenue YoY, a +65.64-point GAAP operating-margin change, and +31.1% trailing 63-session momentum. Its subsequent 252-session price return was −28.54%, with −57.82% path drawdown. In contrast, the June 30, 2025 MU setup had lower +36.56% revenue growth and **negative** growth acceleration, yet the same cached price calculation reports +737.55% subsequently. These price outcomes are supplied by the owning panel analysis; they are not sourced from issuer releases or causally attributed to accounting adjustments. The first setup's extreme margin change compared with a trough quarter carrying a $1.430bn inventory write-down. The stronger headline inflection did not protect against a major subsequent loss. This paired counterexample is a reason to test quality, cycle age and expectations before adopting an earnings-acceleration rule.

STX's October 2023 release described modest U.S. cloud recovery, and its January 2024 release reported improving cloud-nearline demand and positive adjusted EPS. YoY revenue remained negative until its July 2024 release. This suggests different detection dates for narrative recovery, sequential improvement, positive earnings and positive YoY growth. All four should remain separate measures, without selecting the date that maximizes subsequent returns. [October release](https://investors.seagate.com/news/news-details/2023/Seagate-Technology-Reports-Fiscal-First-Quarter-2024-Financial-Results/default.aspx), [January release](https://investors.seagate.com/news/news-details/2024/Seagate-Technology-Reports-Fiscal-Second-Quarter-2024-Financial-Results/default.aspx), [July release](https://investors.seagate.com/news/news-details/2024/Seagate-Technology-Reports-Fiscal-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2024-Financial-Results/default.aspx)

## Earnings and cash-flow quality

The two issuers' adjusted earnings exclude recurring SBC and use issuer-specific diluted-share adjustments. They are not a uniform economic earnings definition. MU's fiscal Q3 2025 adjusted OI excludes $263m SBC and $57m patent-license charges. MU adjusted FCF subtracts capex net of government incentives; it is not simply CFO minus gross equipment spending. These differences are explicitly preserved. [MU release reconciliation](https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2025/Micron-Technology-Inc--Reports-Results-for-the-Third-Quarter-of-Fiscal-2025-06-25-2025/default.aspx)

STX fiscal Q3 2023 GAAP operating loss included a $300m BIS penalty payable over five years. A later YoY margin jump can reflect the absence of this charge as well as business improvement. Fiscal Q4 2024 GAAP NI $513m included a $313m divestiture gain; $226m divestiture proceeds were classified in both CFO and FCF. A simple diagnostic subtraction reduces that quarter's CFO from $434m to $208m and FCF from $380m to $154m. Those are analyst diagnostic calculations, not restated company measures or complete recurring-profit adjustments. [BIS-quarter release](https://investors.seagate.com/news/news-details/2023/Seagate-Technology-Reports-Fiscal-Third-Quarter-2023-Financial-Results/), [divestiture-quarter release](https://investors.seagate.com/news/news-details/2024/Seagate-Technology-Reports-Fiscal-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2024-Financial-Results/default.aspx)

## What can transfer, and what remains unproven

As an **inference**, both firms belong to a broader economic pattern: depressed comparison bases, improved end-market demand, constrained supply/cost discipline and an advantageous product mix can lift operating profitability faster than revenue. The physical drivers differ. MU has DRAM/HBM and NAND pricing, bit shipments and process costs; STX has nearline cloud capacity, areal-density/HAMR products, production constraints and price execution. Grouping them as correlated portfolio exposure is reasonable; treating their contemporaneous earnings improvements as one interchangeable commodity signal is not established by the record.

The next transferable experiment should retain continuous measures for revenue growth, change in growth, GAAP margin change, adjustment burden, company-guided sequential growth, same-quarter management-guide beat and time since the first observable inflection. Apply the same definitions to qualified winners and losers across industries. Avoid a hard positive-growth-acceleration gate: it would exclude both winning June 2025 setups. Rules and sample inclusion must be fixed before examining the next evaluation period.

Historical analyst consensus, forecast-revision vintages and institutional accumulation were **not reconstructed**. Next-quarter guide sequences change the target fiscal quarter and therefore are not same-quarter guidance revisions. No full transcript/Q&A packet was collected. Twenty events from two retrospectively selected winners do not establish breadth, causality, an untouched holdout, or a probability of future 5× outcomes.
