# WDC: earlier earnings acceleration with point-in-time economic scope

The recovery was observable well before the June 30, 2025 selection. The strongest verified earlier operating evidence appears in the April 25 and July 31, 2024 releases. This is a historical, outcome-selected case study; it does not establish a general selection rule or profitable earlier trade.

Ten original quarter releases from January 2023 through April 2025 are preserved in `quarter_timeline.csv`, together with SEC acceptance times, release and filing eligibility dates, measurement scope and formulas. `source_evidence.json` retains separate revised vintages and quality flags. No original research files or deployed trading code were changed.

The January 25, 2024 release linked cloud growth to nearline shipments and pricing; HDD segment margin had improved 4.1 points YoY while its revenue remained below the prior year. The next-quarter adjusted EPS guide moved to −$0.10–+$0.20. This is an early monitoring signal, with continuing negative earnings and cash flow. It is not evidence of analyst estimate revisions. [Original January release](https://investor.wdc.com/news-releases/news-release-details/western-digital-reports-fiscal-second-quarter-2024-financial)

On April 25, 2024, combined revenue was $3,457m versus $2,803m, operating income $273m versus −$472m, and operating cash flow $58m versus −$381m. HDD gross margin rose 6.8 points YoY. This verifies recovery without borrowing later recasts. The formal filing separately reported HDD exabyte growth of 25% and a 6% decrease in price per gigabyte, partly from larger capacity mix. Nearline price per unit and price per gigabyte are different measures. [April release](https://investor.wdc.com/node/25676), [April 2024 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/106040/000010604024000022/wdc-20240329.htm)

By July 31, 2024, HDD revenue was $2,003m versus $1,295m and segment gross margin 36.1% versus 20.7%. This is earlier verification of HDD operating leverage through segment gross profit. A standalone HDD operating-income series cannot be reconstructed from gross profit without corporate cost allocation. ## Why the June 2025 factor record needs interpretation

At the June 2025 decision, reported HDD continuing revenue grew **30.94%**. The previous quarter's **HDD** revenue growth was **76.23%**. The consistent HDD series therefore decelerated by **45.29 percentage points**, rather than the cached −10.39-point acceleration derived using the previous **combined** growth rate. Revenue levels of $2,409m and $1,367m had already been disclosed as HDD segment revenue in January 2025; this comparison does not require hindsight. [January 2025 release](https://investor.wdc.com/node/26351)

March 2025 continuing GAAP operating margin was 33.13%, versus 5.37% a year earlier: +27.76 points. Removing only the $201m litigation credit reduces current margin to 24.37% and improvement to **19.00 points**. Management-adjusted margins were 25.98% and 8.73%: **+17.25 points**. The operating improvement remains substantial, but the GAAP increase overstates recurring leverage. The $698m tax benefit and $606m retained-Sandisk mark loss further distort EPS. Total consolidated NI $520m/$135m includes discontinued Flash; continuing NI was $772m/−$8m. CFO $508m and company FCF $436m also retain Flash through separation and cannot confirm standalone HDD cash conversion. [April 2025 release](https://investor.wdc.com/node/26681), [May 2025 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/106040/000010604025000026/wdc-20250328.htm)

The economic separation completed February 21, 2025; exchange distribution economics began February 24 in the existing price/portfolio accounting. These dates have distinct purposes. Prior-period standalone continuing OI is first verified in this extraction in the April release; February Investor Day may contain earlier pro forma figures and must be retrieved before claiming April was the first possible disclosure.

## Release vintage and execution controls

All original releases are preliminary and unaudited. The January 2024 quarter initially showed consolidated NI −$268m and EPS −$0.87; the February 12 filing revised them to −$287m/−$0.93 after Flash Ventures accounting corrections. The revised prior-year NI also changed. The initial vintage must remain available until the correction becomes observable. [February 2024 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/106040/000010604024000014/wdc-20231229.htm)

The July 2024 original release showed Q4 operating income $507m/EPS $0.88. The annual filing added a $291m litigation charge, yielding Q4 OI $216m from annual-minus-nine-month subtraction. The October release independently reports Q4 OI $216m/EPS $0.08. Do not retrofit these values to July. The October release warned of another verdict assessment; its subsequently filed Q1 OI/NI/EPS remained unchanged. [Annual filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/106040/000010604024000031/wdc-20240628.htm), [October release](https://investor.wdc.com/node/26101), [October filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/106040/000010604024000040/wdc-20240927.htm)

Exact release timestamps were not obtained. Each release becomes eligible only at the first cached SPY session strictly after its publication date. SEC acceptance timestamps are converted from Eastern time to UTC; the conservative filing schedule waits until the first session after the later of local acceptance date or stated filing date. A filing accepted after 5:30pm may have the next day's filing date. The April release is conservatively usable April 26, 2024; the filing-only policy uses May 1. A monthly engine with a previous-day cutoff may therefore detect the same economics at a later month end. Price momentum, membership and cash eligibility still need separate checks.

## Guidance, uncertainty and next test

Nine original-guide/next-result pairs are recorded. The March 2025 result is blocked from comparison to January's combined-company guide. Historical analyst consensus snapshots were not obtained; guidance beats are not earnings surprises. Primary call transcripts were not retrieved.

The February 12 Investor Day strategy release projected 23% HDD exabyte CAGR through 2028 using internal estimates and reported HAMR testing with two hyperscalers. This is management's forward claim, not realized industry demand. The linked deck was detected as 57 pages, but web tools exposed neither text nor usable screenshot images; the bounded download failed HTTP 403. Its numerical guidance was therefore not used. [Investor Day strategy release](https://www.westerndigital.com/company/newsroom/press-releases/2025/2025-02-12-western-digital-unveiled-go-forward-strategy-at-investor-day-2025), [official event/deck link](https://investor.wdc.com/events/event-details/western-digital-investor-day-2025)

The next defensible experiment is a fixed, cross-company rule distinguishing loss-to-profit recovery from positive-EPS percentage growth, with release-timestamp and filing-only variants. Compare both early entry and waiting for two confirmations across unsuccessful cases and other industries. Keep segment gross margin distinct from operating margin, include Q4 vintages, and require a comparability gate around corporate separations. This reconstruction provides earlier observable dates, not permission to lower a threshold after inspecting WDC's subsequent return.
