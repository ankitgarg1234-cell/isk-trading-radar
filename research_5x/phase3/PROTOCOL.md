## A1 — inclusion, labels and matching

Start with every Phase 2 qualified observation. Preserve censored rows separately. Mature labels are the frozen close-to-close 252-session price returns, distinct from executable portfolio returns. Disjoint analysis labels: exceptional `r >= 1`; moderate `.30 <= r < 1`; flat/modest `-.10 <= r < .30`; material loser `-.30 < r < -.10`; severe loser `r <= -.30`. Also publish the overarching material-loss flag `r < -.10`. Boundary inclusions use unrounded returns. Labels never enter accounting extraction or trading predictors.

Use two matching specifications, declared now: strict exact signal date, known Phase 2 sector proxy, same full-panel size quartile, absolute 63-session return difference <= .20; relaxed exact signal date/known sector, absolute log2 market-cap difference <= 1 and momentum difference <= .30. For each observation select the closest OTHER issuer by sum of scaled absolute log2-size and momentum differences; break ties alphabetically. Outcome labels never determine matches. Publish all eligible pairs, deterministic nearest pairs, balance and unmatched reasons. No forced exact match. Current sector and size estimates are proxies, not verified historical classifications; Unknown sector is unmatched. No historical industry control is implied.

Report all observations, first observed qualified signal per company (including left-censoring and missing outcome), and earliest mature observation per company as a separately labeled sensitivity. Repeated monthly signals are not independent. No independent-trial binomial probabilities. Continuous returns and path drawdowns are primary outcome diagnostics; category summaries are secondary.

## A2/A3 — accounting reconstruction and tests

Freeze definitions before retrieval: CFO = reported net cash from operations; gross capex = reported cash purchases of property/plant/equipment (no undisclosed netting); analyst FCF = CFO minus gross capex; cash conversion CFO/NI and FCF/NI only when NI > 0, with nonpositive earnings flagged; CFO/revenue, FCF/revenue, capex/revenue, SBC/revenue and (NI-CFO)/revenue retain signed numerators. Interest/OI only when OI > 0. GAAP operating margin = OI/revenue, gross margin = GP/revenue; adjusted quantities stay separate. Balance-sheet working capital is not automatically equivalent to cash-flow changes. No universal quality score, binary quality cutoff or new ranking is optimized here.

Use source-accession-specific filings and original issuer releases. An eligibility cutoff is the signal date minus one calendar day; filings must have filing date AND Eastern acceptance date no later than that cutoff. Releases without exact timestamps are available only next session. Never backfill an original release with a later correction. Current APIs are discovery/staging only until numbers are tied to original accession documents. Preserve raw inputs, original/revised vintages and source hashes. SEC taxonomy aliases, dimensions, units, start/end dates and conflicts remain visible. Cash flows are typically YTD: quarterly subtraction requires the same fiscal start, a prior quarter ending 60–125 days earlier, and both source vintages available by the cutoff. Q4 annual-minus-nine-month derivation is separate from the original Q4 release; annual values cannot enter before the annual filing. Do not splice incomparable reporting scopes. CFO/NI must refer to the same entity, scope and period.

Mandatory issuer bridges: WDC HDD/combined/Flash/discontinued and litigation/tax/Sandisk marks; MU inventory charges and reduced inventory cost, patent/tax gains, product mix and GAAP-adjusted differences; STX divestiture gains/receipts and unusual expenses. Original Q4 releases and subsequent annual reconciliations are required where obtainable. Analyze all 39 mature issuers for availability; issuer deep dives are selected by matching/coverage, not favorable future returns. Publish a row for every issuer and failure, with missing evidence marked missing rather than zero.

For source-certified comparable metrics: descriptive Spearman correlation with continuous returns and within-date/sector/size demeaned returns; nearest-pair metric differences versus outcome differences; issuer-balanced summaries; first-signal sensitivity; storage/memory exclusion. Bootstrap whole issuers with fixed seed 73011 (2,000 draws) for descriptive uncertainty, disclose cross-company date dependence. No causal claim, optimized cutoffs, p-value mining or predictive certification from this development sample.

## Scientific stop gates

Gate 1 passes for a broad accounting comparison only if original accession/timestamp/period/scope certification covers >=80% of mature observations and >=80% of mature issuers for CFO, revenue and NI, >=70% for capex and SBC, with at least 10 distinct issuers in cross-outcome nearest pairs. Missingness by outcome/sector/date must be published. These are minimum feasibility requirements, not statistical power guarantees. Sparse source-reviewed case evidence is still publishable as case evidence. If vintage or scope cannot be certified, metrics are audit-only and the broad gate fails.

Gate 2 requires incremental quality information surviving issuer-first, date/sector/size controls, storage exclusion and plausible source assumptions, with enough pair/cycle coverage to distinguish quality from industry/date exposure. Even a pass is only promising on reused history. A failed Gate 1 or unresolved Gate 2 stops new B/C strategy optimization. Preserve negative results. No attractive backtest may substitute for either gate.
