What the original statements establish

Micron’s early recovery was less clean than its headline profit suggested. FY2024 Q2 reported OI $191m included a $200m patent gain and $382m of lower costs from previously written-down inventory. Excluding only those two effects gives diagnostic OI −$391m; CFO less gross PPE was −$165m. In June 2025 gross-PPE FCF was +$1,671m. These are source-backed financial distinctions, with incomplete recurring-profit normalization; they are not an independently validated return predictor. MU original-vintage audit.

Cash can be supported by a transaction or a different definition. STX Q4 FY 2024 CFO $434m included $226m divestiture operating receipts; removing only those receipts gives CFO $208m and FCF $154m. The $313m disposal earnings gain was below OI, so subtracting it from OI would be wrong. WDC’s June 2025 income describes continuing HDD but its cash still includes Flash; standalone cash conversion stays missing. WDC’s original Q4 OI $507m/NI $330m and later annual-derived OI $216m/NI $39m remain distinct publication vintages. STX classification bridge, WDC scope/vintage bridge.

Rejected: positive cash conversion is sufficient to prevent material stock losses. DXCM, DECK September 2024, TTD and DASH all had comparable positive CFO and gross-PPE FCF before subsequently losing more than 30% in the frozen stock labels. This rejects a sufficient-condition claim; it does not reject incremental cash-quality information after proper controls.
