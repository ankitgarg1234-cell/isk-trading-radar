# Source inventory and scientific blockers

## What was obtained

The parent commit `4ea4401` is verified remotely. All 105 inputs/results/code files listed in its publication manifest match locally. Prior research is read-only. The complete Phase 2 report, protocol, log, validation status, winning-account audit, source findings, panel and retention comparisons were reviewed before the Phase 3 freeze.

All 379 qualified stock-month records are retained: 188 mature price-only labels across 39 issuers and 191 censored/missing labels across the broader 66-issuer qualified population. These labels are not annual portfolio total returns. The matching inventory publishes strict and relaxed eligible/nearest pairs, unmatched cases, and first-company-only matching. Sector is a current proxy; historical industry and company-size certification are unavailable. Some strict size-quartile pairs differ more than 30× in market capitalization. Relaxed matches explicitly cap the size ratio at 2× but still leave 112 mature observations unmatched.

Public SEC Company Facts and Submissions snapshots were obtained for all 39 mature issuers, plus older META submissions needed for 2024. Selection uses the exact accession, reported period, units and a conservative previous-day filing cutoff. Snapshot files, retrieval inventory, hashes and selected-fact lineage are preserved. The current API is discovery/staging, not an original earnings-release vintage certificate. [SEC API definitions](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) state that the facts API includes standard taxonomy/entity-wide facts; custom metrics and many issuer-specific adjustment details need original documents.

The staging layer covers 188 observations and 91 selected filings. CFO/revenue/NI/SBC values are present for 185 observations (98.4%, 38 issuers); gross PPE cash purchases for 163 (86.7%, 33 issuers). LVS cash requires additional taxonomy/source work. Presence does not imply comparable accounting. Derived quarterly cash requires exact fiscal-start alignment and a previously published YTD operand; original Q4 releases remain distinct from annual-minus-nine-month filing figures.

Original source packets cover mandatory MU/STX/WDC, outcome-blind matched peers NVDA/ZBRA/PLTR/NOW/SNPS/CCL/RCL, and fixed failure cases DXCM/DECK/FSLR/TTD/APP/DASH. Their scope, periods, exact missingness, original snapshots where successfully obtained, source URLs and arithmetic checks are in `accounting_quality/issuer_audits/`. This is partial issuer case coverage, not certification of every monthly observation for those issuers. Original Q4 and annual reconciliations were added instead of inheriting the old Q4 omission. No full historical transcript, analyst-consensus or forecast-valuation series was obtained.

## What prevents Gate 1 certification

1. Source-accession metadata alone does not certify that cash, earnings and revenue have the same economic scope. Consolidated cash can be incorrectly paired with parent-attributable NI or continuing income. WDC HDD cash remains inseparable from Flash in the June 2025 release; APP’s Apps divestiture also changes scope. Those ratios remain missing. NVDA’s combined PPE/intangible purchases cannot certify pure PPE capex.
2. Original accession indices disagree with current SEC submissions UTC strings in reviewed cases. Across 91 selected accessions, 58 disagree with frozen cache timestamps by four/five hours. This is a conflict inventory, not proof that either bulk source is authoritative. Manually reviewed original indices confirm specific conflicts. Exact broad-source acceptance certification is unfinished; no silent timestamp repair or lookahead-permitting fallback is used.
3. Full original-release and filing reconciliation for all 39 issuers/188 observations is not complete. A release can precede its matching cash filing: DECK’s 24 October 2024 release is observable at the 30 October cutoff, while its 31 October filing is not. Pairing latest earnings with stale quarterly cash would create a false quality signal.
4. A “cash-flow fact” may include customer deposits, deferred revenue, government-credit collections, divestiture receipts, factoring and other working-capital timing. Recurring versus temporary cash requires issuer-specific source bridges. SBC cash-flow addbacks are not necessarily the same amounts as non-GAAP expense adjustments. Company-defined FCF is not a common cross-issuer definition.
5. There are only 39 first-company mature observations, including left-edge censoring; first-only matching supports very few comparable pairs. Historical industry and size controls are unresolved. A bootstrap cannot supply missing independent industry cycles or correct these definitions.

The protocol minimum is 80% original source/scope/timestamp certification for CFO/revenue/NI across observations AND issuers, 70% for capex/SBC, and sufficient cross-outcome issuer pairs. API availability meets numerical presence levels but does not meet certification. Gate 1 is therefore **Blocked**, not passed. No cohort-wide accounting alpha statistic is produced from the uncertified staging ratios. Gate 2 incremental predictive evidence is untested; new B/C optimization stops as required by the mandate.

## Independent validation and source cost

Yahoo remains the user-selected price source. [The yfinance cash-flow interface](https://ranaroussi.github.io/yfinance/reference/api/yfinance.Ticker.get_cashflow.html) exposes statements, but it does not certify the historical publication vintage required here. The previously retrieved 2010–2023 prices cover selected surviving cases; failed WBA/PXD probes and the incomplete delisted universe remain in Phase 2. They cannot support an independent historical screen.

The [official S&P 500 page](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/indices/equity/sp-500/) provides index information/current constituent access; this run obtained no complete, dated historical membership export. Complete historical member IDs, sector/industry changes, acquisitions/delisting proceeds and dividend payable dates remain unresolved. Original corporate-action economics require issuer-level reconciliation rather than assuming an adjusted Yahoo close is an executable price.

SEC/issuer public documents have no data purchase cost in this run; intermittent SEC/IR HTTP403 failures and current-page versus historical-archive limitations are recorded. No licensed database was purchased. Commercial membership/delist/consensus pricing and access are **not obtained**, rather than guessed. No new provider access is implied. A prospective research-paper validation protocol is provided; it is a document only and changes no account.

Expand historical industry/size controls and publish the unmatched subset. Only then evaluate frozen quality measures with issuer-first/date/sector/size and storage-exclusion sensitivities. Obtain a dated historical security master and terminal proceeds, or prospectively collect original snapshots before any future returns are observed. Freeze a new evaluation protocol before using that independent data.
