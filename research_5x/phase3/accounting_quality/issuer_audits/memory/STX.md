# STX — cash conversion and earnings contain different divestiture effects

Amounts are USD millions. The original fiscal Q4 2023 and 2024 releases are retained separately from the corresponding annual filings; annual revenue, operating income, net income, CFO and gross capex tie out on both years. This targets the mandatory accounting issue and does not estimate causal stock-return attribution.

The July 23, 2024 release reported Q4 operating income 314, GAAP net income 513 and adjusted net income 222. A 313 pre-tax divestiture gain is **below operating income**; removing it from operating income would be incorrect. Operating profitability nevertheless included 26 of purchase-order cancellation credits and 3 of restructuring credits. Removing these credits alone gives analyst diagnostic OI 285, keeping SBC expense. The Q4 adjusted OI 327 includes SBC exclusions and other adjustments; it is not a complete recurring economic-profit measure.

CFO was 434 and gross capex 54, yielding company FCF 380. Of 560 gross divestiture cash proceeds, 326 was investing cash and 226, net of transaction costs, was operating cash. Removing those operating receipts gives CFO 208 and FCF 154. The 313 accounting gain and 226 cash receipts are different quantities. Subtracting a pre-tax gain from net income without an item-level tax bridge would not certify recurring net income or a clean cash/earnings ratio. [Original FY2024 Q4 release](https://investors.seagate.com/news/news-details/2024/Seagate-Technology-Reports-Fiscal-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2024-Financial-Results/default.aspx)

The original annual filing verifies FY2024 CFO 918, gross capex 254, net income 335 and the divestiture classification. Analyst CFO/FCF excluding only the divestiture receipts are 692/438; this does not remove all unusual cash flows. The filing also describes increased receivable factoring, payment timing, inventory accumulation, 25 of swap cash receipts and 45 cash paid against the earlier BIS penalty. The disposal did not qualify as discontinued operations. Annual statements therefore continue to include the sold operation for its owned period, rather than presenting a newly separated-company series. FY2024 interest expense 332 against operating income 452 shows substantial financing burden; improved OI alone does not imply low balance-sheet risk. [Original FY2024 10-K, including Note 18](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1137789/000113778924000068/stx-20240628.htm)

FY2023 CFO 942 remained positive while NI was −529; CFO/NI is intentionally missing because the earnings denominator is nonpositive. Working-capital liquidation and 110 of swap receipts supported cash. The 300 BIS accrual was an expense before later cash installments. Positive CFO through a downturn is not automatically evidence of recurring demand recovery. [Original FY2023 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1137789/000113778923000049/stx-20230630.htm)

Q4 2023 CFO 218 minus gross capex 50 yields FCF 168; annual CFO 942 less 316 yields 626. This simple gross-capex definition matches Seagate's company FCF but differs materially from Micron's adjusted FCF and WDC's net-capex/Flash Ventures measure. Cross-issuer comparisons require the frozen common definition, then issuer-specific cash-quality diagnostics.
