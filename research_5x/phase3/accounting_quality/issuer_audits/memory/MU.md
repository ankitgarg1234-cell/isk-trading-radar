# MU — profitable recovery can still contain temporary accounting support

These are mandatory issuer controls, not an outcome-selected forecasting rule. All amounts below are USD millions. The packet retains the original fiscal Q4 2023 and 2024 releases, then ties annual revenue, operating income, net income, CFO and gross PPE spending to their original annual filings. The four FY2024 quarterly income/cash totals also reconcile to the annual statement. Q4 is not silently omitted.

The March 20, 2024 release reported GAAP operating income 191, adjusted operating income 204, net income 793 and adjusted net income 476. GAAP operating income included a 200 patent cross-license gain. Both profitability bases retained 382 of lower inventory cost from inventory written down previously. Removing only the patent gain gives operating income −9; removing that gain and lower inventory cost gives −391; adjusted operating income minus lower inventory cost is −178. These are **analyst diagnostics**, not complete recurring-profit restatements. Lower cost on the sale of previously impaired inventory is not a GAAP write-up reversal. The tax-method catch-up also affected both GAAP and adjusted net income, unequally. CFO 1,219 was positive, but CFO minus gross PPE spending 1,384 was −165, versus company adjusted FCF −29. [Original FY2024 Q2 release](https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2024/Micron-Technology-Inc--Reports-Results-for-the-Second-Quarter-of-Fiscal-2024-03-20-2024/default.aspx)

The June 25, 2025 release reported operating income 2,169, adjusted operating income 2,490, net income 1,885 and CFO 4,609. Gross PPE spending 2,938 gives analyst FCF 1,671; the company measure 1,949 adds PPE sale proceeds 12 and government incentives 266. The OI adjustment consists of SBC 263, patent-license expense 57 and other 1. Positive reported cash and a smaller adjustment dependence support a stronger accounting picture than the early 2024 inflection. This comparison does not control for entry valuation, expectations, cycle position or market conditions and cannot establish a predictive advantage.
