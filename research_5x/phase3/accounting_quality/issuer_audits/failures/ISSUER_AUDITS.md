## Dexcom

The [April25 original release](https://investors.dexcom.com/news/news-details/2024/Dexcom-Reports-First-Quarter-2024-Financial-Results/default.aspx) disclosed rising operating profit but falling gross margin and a tax benefit. Adjusted OI140.2 exceeded GAAP101.1; adjusted NI128.2 was below GAAP146.4 after the57.3 tax adjustment. The [original Q1 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1093557/000109355724000079/dxcm-20240331.htm), accepted17:17:02ET April25, supports209.2 CFO,56.9 PPE purchases and39.0 SBC. Historical valuation/consensus and future commercial execution remain unattributed.

## Deckers

The [July25 release](https://ir.deckers.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/237/deckers-brands-reports-first-quarter-fiscal-year-2025-financial-results) and [August1 Q1 filing](https://ir.deckers.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0000910521-24-000042/deck-20240630.htm) support an actual profit improvement, modest SBC and positive FCF. Cash profit conversion slipped as seasonal inventory/payable flows offset income. [October24 Q2 results](https://ir.deckers.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/234/deckers-brands-reports-second-quarter-fiscal-year-2025-financial-results) strengthened operating income again, but the [Q2 filing](https://ir.deckers.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0000910521-24-000059/deck-20240930.htm) was accepted11:40:56ET October31, after the October30 cutoff. It cannot supply Q2 cash conversion at that signal. Earnings per share in the October release reflect the six-for-one split, while July original EPS remains on its original share basis. No accounting restatement is inferred from a mechanical split. Clean accounting evidence and brand profitability did not prevent losses; missing valuation and demand expectations stop a causal attribution.

## Exact missingness and evidentiary limit

Seven cases have original eligible income-release lineage. Six have quarter CFO/PPE evidence, while DECK October31 lacks eligible Q2 CFO/PPE/SBC. Only five cases have directly matching consolidated CFO/NI and CFO/revenue; APP has a documented consolidated revenue/NI bridge but continuing-only conversion remains missing. No adjusted OI/NI is inferred from adjusted EBITDA. Gross margin is missing for TTD. Complete recurring-profit adjustments, cash-flow/working-capital history, industry classification, analyst consensus and valuation snapshots are not certified across the qualified universe. These deep dives cannot pass the broad80% accounting gate or establish a factor's incremental predictive power.

The supported negative conclusion is narrow: improving GAAP operating profit and positive CFO/FCF are not sufficient to prevent a material future stock loss. This does not reject cash conversion as an incremental factor. Poor cash quality, nonrecurring gains, and reporting changes are heterogeneous diagnostics; their causal contribution and transferable predictive value remain unproven.
