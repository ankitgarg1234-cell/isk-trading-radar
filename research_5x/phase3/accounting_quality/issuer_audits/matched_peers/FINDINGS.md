# Matched-peer original-source accounting audit

Status: **bounded original-source case evidence; incremental predictive value untested; independent validation blocked**. Prepared 11 October 2026 under the frozen Phase 3 protocol. This packet covers seven peer companies, eight latest-quarter report vintages, nine stock-month observations and five supplemental annual/YTD windows. MU/WDC/STX source bridges belong to the separate mandatory issuer audit; this packet does not replace them.

The matching was fixed without using subsequent returns. Every source used by the nine case observations passes the previous-calendar-day cutoff using original SEC index filing dates and Eastern acceptance clocks. Release eligibility conservatively starts on the next actual SPY session after the release date. No orders or portfolio simulations were created. The preserved stock labels are 252-session close-to-close **price** returns; they are not 12-calendar-month total account returns, and WDC labels span a separation. Future outcome labels were joined only after accounting extraction.

## Findings that survive the source audit

**Positive cash conversion is not sufficient to identify future winners.** At the first observed ZBRA qualification, Q3 CFO was $294m, gross PPE spending $17m, protocol FCF $277m and consolidated CFO/NI 2.146. Its frozen subsequent stock return was −31.84%, with −49.29% path drawdown. The later ZBRA observation reuses the same report and is not a second independent failure. PLTR also had positive GAAP OI, positive CFO and positive protocol FCF before its −7.77% and +4.18% stock outcomes. These counterexamples reject a guarantee or sufficient-condition interpretation; they do not reject a possible incremental statistical effect. Original source: [Zebra Q3 exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/877212/000087721224000162/zbraex99120240928.htm), [Palantir Q1 exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1321655/000132165525000063/a2025q1ex991pressrelease.htm), [Q2 exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1321655/000132165525000105/a2025q2ex991pressrelease.htm).

**The NOW/SNPS comparison supplies one plausible cash warning, not a transferable result.** NOW's original Q4 CFO/NI was 5.441 and protocol FCF $1,344m; SNPS CFO/NI was −0.197 and FCF −$128.181m despite positive consolidated NI. Their later price outcomes were +17.47% and −22.36%. But NOW's Q4 CFO includes a $1,302m deferred-revenue contribution, while SNPS has a 14-week quarter and another business mix. The one matched date cannot disentangle cash timing, billing seasonality, industry demand or expectations. Original sources: [NOW Q4 exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1373715/000137371524000009/erq4fy23.htm), [SNPS Q1 exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883241/000119312524041524/d759623dex991.htm).

**Consolidated earnings and parent-attributable earnings must remain separate.** PLTR Q1 consolidated NI is $217.717m versus parent NI $214.031m; Q2 is $328.572m versus $326.727m. SNPS is $446.207m versus $449.112m; RCL Q3 is $1,010.678m versus $1,009.076m. For these originals, `us-gaap:ProfitLoss` matches the consolidated cash-flow starting income and `us-gaap:NetIncomeLoss` can refer to parent-attributable income. This is a source-specific finding, not a claim that one taxonomy tag always has a fixed economic scope. A CFO ratio using the generic API earnings field is not automatically certified. [PLTR Q1 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1321655/000132165525000066/pltr-20250331.htm), [SNPS filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883241/000088324124000013/snps-20240131.htm), [RCL filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/884887/000088488723000056/rcl-20230930.htm).

**Issuer “free cash flow” definitions are materially different.** PLTR adds back cash payroll taxes: Q1 issuer FCF $370.377m versus protocol $304.079m; Q2 $568.769m versus $531.617m. CCL FY23 original-release adjusted FCF $2,146m includes $1,157m export credits; protocol CFO less gross PPE purchases is $989m. NOW annual issuer FCF $2,728m adds back $24m acquisition costs over protocol $2,704m. NVDA combines PPE and intangible purchases and subtracts financing principal in issuer FCF; a pure gross-PPE protocol FCF cannot be certified. Definitions and missing denominators are retained separately. [CCL original release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/815097/000081509723000071/a20234qearningsrelease8-k.htm), [NOW original release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1373715/000137371524000009/erq4fy23.htm), [NVDA original release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581024000028/q4fy24pr.htm).

**An annual vintage difference must not rewrite an earlier quarterly release.** CCL's 21 December release reports FY23 CFO $4,273m; its original 26 January 10-K reports $4,281m. Both were available before the 30 January cutoff, but the quarterly release CFO remains $915m. A later annual-minus-nine-month calculation of $923m is not certified without a same-vintage nine-month bridge. The reason for the $8m annual difference has not been established here. [CCL annual filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/815097/000081509724000011/ccl-20231130.htm).

**Current SEC API acceptance metadata cannot be used unconditionally as an original clock.** Ten of the 17 preserved SEC accessions show four- or five-hour differences between current `acceptanceDateTime` and the original index time converted from Eastern to UTC. The discrepancy is recorded per source in `source_lineage.csv`; no API correction is silently inferred. These particular case signals are later than both clocks, so case inclusion does not change. PLTR filing dates fall on the day after their evening acceptance; the protocol requires both dates to pass. Original index examples: [NVDA](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581024000028/0001045810-24-000028-index.html), [PLTR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1321655/000132165525000063/0001321655-25-000063-index.html).

## Matching, repetitions and failure attribution

Seven fixed pair-date comparisons cover nine distinct issuers including parent-audited MU/WDC. All pass the strict sector/quartile/momentum proxy specification. Only four pass the relaxed continuous-size specification: CCL/RCL, NOW/SNPS and the two WDC/ZBRA months. No pair has certified historical industry controls. WDC/ZBRA and PLTR/MU repeat companies, industries and label windows; they are not independent trials. Four of the nine peer signal observations are first-observed/earliest-mature company observations: ZBRA October, SNPS, CCL and RCL. First observed in this panel is left-censored and does not establish a first-ever qualification or business inflection. Within the selected peer packet, the severe ZBRA cash-positive failure and the negative-NI CCL moderate winner therefore survive that sensitivity. This is not a complete first-signal cohort estimate; missing first reports for the other peer cases are not replaced by their later available observations.

## Coverage and scientific decisions

For the eight unique latest-quarter reports, revenue/OI/NI are available 8/8, CFO 7/8, gross capex/SBC 6/8, comparable directly reported adjusted OI 4/8, adjusted parent NI 7/8, issuer FCF 6/8 and gross interest expense 3/8. Repeated ZBRA observations do not increase unique coverage. Supplemental annual and nine-month windows are excluded from these quarter denominators. Complete row-level missingness is in `missing_data_coverage.csv`; do not treat it as all-39-issuer coverage.

The packet's source/hash/denominator/cutoff arithmetic checks pass. **The broad Phase 3 Gate 1 still does not pass:** this sparse packet neither certifies 80% of the 188 mature observations/39 issuers nor supplies ten distinct comparable cross-outcome issuers with historical industry/size controls. A sparse case audit cannot substitute for the parent's unresolved full-cohort vintage and scope certification. Gate 2 incremental association is consequently untested; entry/exit optimization must remain stopped.

- **Rejected:** cash positivity/high cash conversion guarantees exceptional subsequent returns, or vendor earnings/FCF fields can be treated as economically interchangeable without original scope/definition checks.
- **Promising but unvalidated:** recurring SBC, non-operating gains, tax benefits, gross-capex cash conversion and working-capital seasonality may improve failure diagnosis. The NOW/SNPS comparison is one illustration and is not predictive validation.
- **Blocked:** broad matched/cohort incremental factor test; first-ever recovery and consecutive-release milestones; reliable vintage-adjusted forward valuation/expectations; historical industry control; independent-cycle portfolio evaluation.
- **Validated:** no investment characteristic or repeatable portfolio policy is independently validated by this reused sample. Original-source numerical reconciliations are verified accounting facts, not strategy validation.

Next work is ranked by feasibility: certify additional unsuccessful original accessions and their consolidated NI scope; retrieve RCL Q2 and CCL nine-month same-vintage bridges; resolve CCL's annual CFO difference; reconstruct complete earlier release sequences; obtain historical sector/industry, dual-listed economic size and expectations vintages. Continue to preserve public-source access denials. Do not add thresholds or simulate policies to compensate for these gaps.

## Reproduction and files

Run `extract_original_facts.py`, then `build_packet.py`, then `test_case_audit.py` with Python and lxml. The append-only `collect_sources.py` is only needed to refresh or replace missing raw downloads; reproducibility of the recorded evidence does not depend on a fresh network fetch. All paths are relative to this directory. Parent Phase 3 cohort and existing SPY dates are read-only inputs.

`download_manifest.json` retains 57 attempts, including 20 failed attempts. Thirty-seven original-source artifacts are preserved: 20 earnings releases/filings/IR pages and 17 SEC index pages. The original NOW IR PDF remains unavailable; its successful SEC exhibit is the accounting source. SHA256 hashes refer to uncompressed original bytes. `original_inline_facts.csv` contains 7,598 raw facts, including 115 unparsed and 3,761 dimensional rows; those staging rows are not universal quality certification. Manual original-release table review remains explicit in `metric_lineage.csv`.

`curated_evidence.json` preserves the original values, supplemental windows, cash subtractions and notes. `normalized_metrics.csv` computes only frozen accounting definitions. `source_lineage.csv` records original/API timestamp disagreements. `signal_case_matrix.csv`, `matched_pair_diagnostics.csv`, `failure_attribution.csv` and `missing_data_coverage.csv` publish case scope and failure diagnostics. `verification_results.json` reports meaningful integrity checks and explicitly blocked scientific gates. No production code, accounts, positions, broker connections or prior research were changed.
